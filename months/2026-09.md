## What happened

**Week 1 (9/16–20):** Strong start, then the budgeting broke on the weekend. A "mild" Friday picked up ~500 kcal at a snack table; Saturday ran 4,077 with ~2,400 of it after the bar.

**Week 2 (9/21–27):** Five of the cleanest days of the month (avg ~2,100, protein 183–208 g). Then Saturday 4,244 and Sunday 2,871 with only 95 g protein. First barbell squat session on 9/23 plus the weekend pushed the scale to 179.6.

**Week 3 start (9/28–30):** Clean recovery. Scale peaked at 181.6 on Monday, then dropped 4 lb in two days as the water cleared.

## What we learned

1. **Weekdays are solved.** Meal prep, protein, and steps run on autopilot Monday–Friday.
2. **Weekends are the whole game.** Three heavy social days added roughly 4,500 kcal over plan, about two weeks' worth of weekday deficit.
3. **The overruns are late-night food, not drinks.** Drinks were defensible both weekends.
4. **Days grow past their planned size.** A "light" day became a heavy one twice. Naming the day's size in advance is the fix.
5. **Maintenance is probably ~2,400–2,500, not 2,900.** The trend held flat on ~2,470/day. Low confidence until a clean stretch confirms it.
6. **Scale noise is big for Mike.** Swings of 3–4 lb in 48 hours after carb/sodium/alcohol days. Only the trend gets a reaction.
7. **The daily check-in works.** He said so, and logging held 15/15 days.
